Troubleshooting
Before startingStand in the Gravity Chamber bot room. Alive or dead is fine.
Won't startOpen Roblox first. F6 only starts a run while Roblox is the active window.
Lost focusLosing focus pauses the run. Return to Roblox and it carries on; the status reads Paused meanwhile.
No ultsCheck the ult slot (7 or 8) in Settings and that the move is equipped. Auto falls back to Ki blasts.
